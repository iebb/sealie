03 / ABOUT THE TYPEFACE

Old structures.
Open possibilities.

Small Seal carries a language of curves, balance, and carefully held space. Sealie follows those structures with a shared round pen and a continuous weight axis, designed for type specimens, editorial experiments, and new ways of reading an old script.

All 11,328 characters in the Unicode 18 Small Seal repertoire are included. The font is developed by ieb with AI-assisted drawing and review, and editable SVG sources are available in the repository.

The Regular 400 design has recorded visual review. Reference comparisons and further optical refinements are maintained with the open sources.
